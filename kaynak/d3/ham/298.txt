Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher designs an activity named Spin and Name the Food using one of the gamification web
tools. Then the teacher prepares an animation titled Interview about Eating Habits using one of the
animation creation tools (Annex-1). Lastly, for the assessment, the teacher creates an activity titled
Healthy or Unhealthy using one of the web tools for matching activities (Annex-2).
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the Spin and Name the Food activity, where the students spin the wheel, look at
the visual and name the foods, such as egg, milk, tomato, etc. The students who give the correct answer
can say whether they like the given food or not.
While Activity
Subsequent to the activity, the teacher introduces the Interview about Eating Habits animation, during
which the students are required to identify appropriate phrases about food preferences, express
opinions regarding liked and disliked foods, and categorise foods as healthy or unhealthy (Annex-1).
Upon completion of the animation, the teacher poses the question, “What kinds of foods do you prefer
for breakfast?” prompting the students to construct sentences expressing their food preferences and
aversions. Additionally, the students are tasked with formulating sentences that provide examples of
healthy and unhealthy foods. The students are expected to utilise structures such as “I prefer,” “I like/
dislike,” or “I enjoy/hate” when discussing food, and the teacher provides immediate feedback throughout
the activity.
Post-Activity
After the animation, to assess the comprehension, the teacher implements the Healthy or Unhealthy
matching activity, where the students are asked to classify the given foods under the topics of healthy
or unhealthy (Annex-2).
Before the Lesson
TThe teacher selects an age-appropriate song such as “Yummy Breakfast” to energise the students.
Additionally, for the assessment, the teacher is required to prepare a Self-Assessment Form using one of
the web tools for self-assessment (Annex-3).

2. Ders ( IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the “Yummy Breakfast” song, where the students are required to listen to the song,
dance and clap their hands when they hear the name of food.
While Activity
Following the song, the teacher asks the students to work in groups of three to create an interview video
using one of the video editing tools, where the students are required to write an interview about their
eating habits and record their interview. Additionally, the students are encouraged to mention the foods
they like or dislike, as well as identify healthy and unhealthy options while creating their interview video.
The teacher informs the students that they will evaluate their own performance at the end of the lesson.
While the students are conducting their interviews, the teacher circulates around the classroom to
monitor their progress. If the students require assistance, the teacher offers guidance and encourages
them to approach the task with creativity and productivity. When the students complete recording the
video, the teacher asks the students to share their videos on EBA and as an assignment, the students
are required to comment on the videos of their classmates.
Post-Activity
When the students finish creating the interview video, the teacher shares the Self-Assessment Form and
wants the students to evaluate their own performances (Annex-3). The teacher discusses the results of
the assessment with the students and provides the necessary feedback.
Before the Lesson
The teacher asks the students to complete Vocabulary Exercise 3: Yummy Breakfast activity on EBA
and designs various digital materials to enhance student engagement and learning. First, the teacher
develops an activity named Find the Foods using one of the gamification web tools (Annex-4). Then
the teacher designs a presentation titled Food Labels using one of the interactive presentation tools
(Annex-5). Lastly, for the assessment, the teacher creates an escape game titled Nutritious Kitchen
using one of the gamified measurement and evaluation web tools (Annex-6).
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the Find the Foods activity, where the students look at the given words and
find them in the word grid (Annex-4). A student who identifies a given word is required to construct a
sentence containing it.

While Activity
Following the activity, the teacher initiates the Food Labels presentation, where the students are presented
with the phrases to label the foods (Annex-5). During the presentation, the teacher poses questions such
as “Do you pay attention to product labels?” and “What is the expiry date? Is it important, and why?” to
maintain student engagement and provides immediate feedback throughout the activity.
Post-Activity
Following the presentation, the students work in pairs to assess their comprehension. The teacher then
implements the Nutritious Kitchen escape game, in which the students follow the provided steps and
answer the interactive questions to complete the task and reach the exit (Annex-7).
