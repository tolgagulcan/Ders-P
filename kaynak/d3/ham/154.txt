## 5. Sinif/Ingilizce/Life in the World - Eat Healthy, Stay Healthy.pdf
SURE:3 KOD:ENG.5.6.L3. ENG.5.6.P1. ENG.5.6.R2. ENG.5.6.R3.
KAV:Food events, Healthy life, Ordering in a restaurant
Learning Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a game named Tap and Say using one of the gamified measurement and evaluation
web tools. Then the teacher prepares a video titled Healthy Eating Day using one of the video editing tools
(Annex-1). Lastly, for the assessment, the teacher designs an activity named Question Set using one of the
web tools for instant feedback (Annex-2).
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the Tap and Say activity on the interactive whiteboard, where the students come to the
board in pairs, tap each box to open, and say the corresponding name for the visuals, such as beef, beans,
and soup. As a consequence of this activity, the students recognise the target words related to the main
components of the current content with the help of the visual and written elements.
While Activity
Following the vocabulary activation stage, the teacher transitions to the Healthy Eating Day video, wherein
the students recognise the clauses/sentences containing the target grammatical chunks in different
contexts (Annex-1). The teacher asks questions about the eating habits of the students and the relationship
between the eating habits and health. Then, the teacher requires the students to watch and listen to the video
content by paying attention to the target phonological aspects, including pronunciation and intonation of
utterances. Upon completion of the video, the teacher chooses five students to read the dialogues aloud,
and in this way, the students gain awareness of clauses/sentences that contain target grammatical chunks
that are presented. To check the comprehension, the teacher initiates the Question Set, in which the students
answer different types of questions related to the video (Annex-2). While answering the questions, the
students make significant inferences that contribute to the understanding of the meaning of the generally
perceived current content.
Post-Activity
Following the questions, the teacher invites the students to work in groups of five to rehearse and act out the
given dialogue in the video. The aim of this activity is to help the students reinforce the target phonological
elements by repeating them several times in groups. The volunteer groups act out the dialogue in front of
the class.

Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a game named Spin and Ask using one of the gamified measurement and evaluation

2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher shares the Spin and Ask activity with the students. In this task, the students work in pairs. One
student spins the wheel and orders the food shown in the visual, while the other student responds appropriately
as a waiter/waitress. The teacher writes an example dialogue on the board for the activity and encourages
the students to use the target vocabulary spontaneously and apply the target grammatical chunks, such
as “Can I have…?”, “Would you like…?” and “Here you are.”
While Activity
Following the vocabulary and grammatical chunks activation stage, the students are invited to work in
groups of four to create a video in a restaurant, using one of the video editing tools. In this task, the students
take on the roles of waiter/waitress and customer and collaboratively design a restaurant menu including
both traditional Turkish meals and various meals from various cuisines. The customers are required to order
food using the target grammatical chunks, such as “Can I have some soup?” or “I’d like some salad, please.”
The waiters/waitresses are expected to describe the ingredients of the dishes and give brief information
about them. This activity aims to enhance the students’ communicative competence by using language
in a real-life context.
Post-Activity
The students are required to upload their videos to EBA. Each group is invited to watch their peers’ videos,
like or comment on the video.The teacher leaves constructive comments and provides feedback.

Before the Lesson
The teacher creates an activity named Match the Pairs using one of the web tools for matching activities.
Then, the teacher crafts a presentation titled Healthy Recipes for Children using one of the interactive
presentation tools (Annex-3). The teacher designs a digital board named Share Your Recipes using one of
the tools for creating digital boards or sticky notes. Lastly, for assessment, the teacher prepares an activity
named Exit Card using one of the visual creation and editing tools (Annex-4).
3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the lesson asking questions such as, “Do you help your parents in the kitchen?”, “Can
you cook?”, “Can you tell the steps of a recipe?” and “Do you enjoy trying different kinds of food?” to engage
the students’ attention. Then, the teacher says, “Let’s revise some phrases we use to tell the steps of a recipe.”
and shares the Match the Pairs activity with the students. In this task, the students work in pairs to match
the audios with the corresponding visuals, such as mixing the ingredients and cutting the onion. This task
aims to help the students recognise significant horizontal/vertical/spiral relationships within the generally
perceived current content, with the help of classifications in line with the new English curriculum outcomes.
While Activity
Following the vocabulary activation stage, the teacher shares the Healthy Recipes for Children presentation,
in which the importance of healthy lifestyle is emphasised, and the steps of some healthy recipes are
provided (Annex-3). The students are required to work in groups of four to review the current content by
examining the surrounding audio/visual materials and identifying the general topic through skimming. After
the students complete skimming the presentation, the teacher asks each group to share their predictions
about the content. Then, the teacher goes over the presentation in detail and discusses the importance of
our eating habits for our health and asks additional questions including: “Do you think the slogan “Healthy
food, healthy mind.” is a good slogan? Why?”, “Can you make a new slogan for healthy eating?” Following
the discussion, the students again work in their groups to prepare a simple and healthy recipe, which they
then share with their classmates. The students may use artificial intelligence powered tools to find ideas for
their recipes. The teacher provides ongoing feedback throughout the course of the activity.
Post-Activity
The teacher shares the link to the digital board, Share Your Recipes, with the students and asks them to
share their group work on the digital board.
