Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher uses a web tool for creating mind maps to create a sample Mind Map with branches for
three holiday destinations: “Rize”, “Antalya”, and “Cappadocia” to facilitate the brainstorming activity.
1. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
To begin the lesson, the teacher reviews holidays and the past tense, ensuring the students are
refreshed on these concepts. The teacher then engages the students in a mind mapping activity
by writing three holiday destinations on the board: “Rize”, “Antalya” and “Cappadocia”. The teacher
draws three branches from each destination and asks the students about holiday activities for each
place. For example, for Rize, the students might suggest rafting, hiking, sailing, and mountain climbing.
For Antalya, activities might include swimming, sunbathing, diving, and visiting historical sites. For
Cappadocia, the students might suggest hot air balloon rides, exploring caves, hiking, and visiting
museums. The teacher writes down these suggestions and encourages the students to share their
ideas. The sample Mind Map is displayed by the teacher.

Holiday Adventure
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher initiates Grammar Exercise 5 and Grammar Exercise 6 on EBA to strengthen the students’
grammatical accuracy when using past tenses, providing guidance throughout the tasks. The
students proceed to complete the exercises collaboratively, offering peer support to ensure a shared
understanding of the material. Following this, the teacher uses an artificial intelligence powered tool
on the interactive whiteboard to generate a 100-word unfinished story related to holiday places,
activities, and the simple past tense. The teacher initiates the artificial intelligence powered tool on
interactive whiteboard and writes this instruction: “Please, write a 100-word unfinished story related to
holiday places, activities and the simple past tense (regular verbs) for 6th grade students.” The teacher
reads the generated text aloud and asks the students to complete the story, providing enough time
for them to work individually. The students are encouraged to collaborate and assist each other with
their stories.
Post-Activity
The students share their completed stories aloud and receive feedback from the teacher and their
peers. This collaborative environment promotes learning and improvement.
Homework/Assignment
The students are assigned to create their own mind map about holiday places and activities at home,
using the web tool for creating mind maps demonstrated earlier in the lesson.
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher reviews the mind maps created by the students in the previous lesson.
The students should be ready to share and discuss their mind maps with the class, explaining their
choices and the activities they included. Criteria to evaluate the students’ writing and reading are
also prepared in advance (Annex-1).
2. Ders ( Class)
Pre-Activity
To start the lesson, the mind maps the students created on holidays are reviewed. Then the students
are divided into small groups to discuss their mind maps and share their ideas. Finally, a few students
from each group present their mind maps to the class, and they identify common themes and discuss
their choices. This activity helps the students recall their previous learning, connect their ideas, and
prepare for the upcoming lesson.

Holiday Adventure
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher initiates Writing Exercise: Colarado on EBA, guiding the students through a shared
reading of the passage and a discussion of the holiday described. Then the teacher tasks them with
composing a well-structured paragraph of 80 to 100 words, reflecting on a memorable holiday from
their own experience. The students are required to read their written output, allowing sufficient time
for the activity. During this activity, the teacher moves between the desks, monitoring the students'
writing process and offering guidance when necessary.
Post-Activity
The students are invited to read their paragraphs aloud. The teacher evaluates the writing process
and the finished paragraphs using predefined criteria: understanding of the task, correct grammar
use, correct use of past tense verbs, the coherence of the text, and fluency in reading. This assessment
takes place both during the writing process and when the students present their work, providing
comprehensive feedback (Annex-1).
Homework/Assignment
The teacher assigns Tour Around the World as an educational activity on EBA. The teacher requests
that the students to take notes on the various holiday activities depicted in the video while it is being
viewed. Additionally, the teacher informs the students that a question and answer activity about the
video will be conducted in the subsequent lesson.
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares the question and answer activity for “Tour Around the World” video assignment
(Annex-2). The teacher also gathers pictures or flashcards of different holiday activities, and a set of
conversation prompt cards related to holidays and past experiences for “Holiday Adventure” game
(Annex-3).
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher begins the lesson by conducting the question and answer activity about Tour Around the
World shared as an assignment on EBA in the previous lesson (Annex-2). The students are encouraged
to share their notes and discuss the key points such as holiday activities and places from the video.
This activity helps to activate prior knowledge and sets the context for the upcoming game.
5
Holiday Adventure
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher explains the “Holiday Adventure” game, which focuses on identifying holiday-related
activities, discussing holidays, and describing past activities and personal experiences (Annex-3). The
game is divided into three parts: Activity Spotting, Holiday Talk, and Past Activities Game. In Activity
Spotting, the class is divided into small groups and shown pictures or flashcards of different holiday
activities. Each group identifies and discusses the activities they see, using descriptive language.
The teacher records the discussions using an audio editing tool, which will be reviewed later in the
lesson. In Holiday Talk, the students use conversation prompt cards to discuss their favourite holidays
and memorable holiday experiences with their group members, sharing details such as locations,
activities, and companions. In the Past Activities Game, the students work in pairs or groups, taking turns
describing past activities or experiences from prompt cards while their partner guesses. Throughout
the game, the teacher monitors and supports the students, noting their use of language, vocabulary,
and grammar for informal observation and feedback.
Post-Activity
After the game, the teacher plays back the audio recordings of the students’ discussions. This allows
the class to listen to their conversations and reflect on their use of language, vocabulary, and grammar.
The teacher leads a brief discussion on what they did well and areas for improvement, providing
constructive feedback to enhance their speaking skills.
