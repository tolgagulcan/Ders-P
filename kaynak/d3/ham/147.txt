Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher is required to design various digital materials to enhance student engagement and
learning. First, the teacher prepares a presentation titled Magical World of Games using one of the
interactive presentation tools (Annex-1). Then, for the assessment, the teacher creates a Balloon Pop
game using one of the gamified measurement and evaluation web tools.
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the “Which game is it?” activity, in which the students act out or mime a game
for their classmates to guess. The teacher then asks for a volunteer student to leave the classroom.
While they are away, the teacher asks the rest of the class to decide on a game to describe. At least
five students can volunteer to leave the classroom, and there can be five different games need to be
mimed.
While Activity
Following the “Which game is it?” activity, the teacher initiates the Magical World of Games presentation,
where the students are asked to identify the names of games and hobbies, as well as phrases related
to likes and dislikes, and abilities and inabilities (Annex-1). During the presentation, the teacher asks,
“What kind of games do you play with your friends?”. The students are encouraged to express their
preferences regarding games they like or don’t like, followed by constructing sentences that reflect
their abilities and inabilities. The students are required to use the structures such as, “I like/dislike” or “I
enjoy/hate” while talking about the games and hobbies and the teacher provides prompt feedback
throughout the course of the activity.
Post-Activity
After the presentation, to assess the comprehension, the teacher implements the Balloon Pop game,
in which the students are asked to look at the images of games and hobbies and match them with the
corresponding terms such as hide and seek, blind man’s buff, hopscotch, etc.

Before the Lesson
The teacher designs various digital materials to boost student motivation. First, the teacher creates a
Word Grid using one of the gamification web tools (Annex-2). Then, the teacher designs a My Weekly
Game Schedule using one of the interactive presentation tools (Annex-3). Lastly, for the assessment,
the teacher prepares a Reflective Journal using one of the poster and flyer creation tools (Annex-4).
2. Ders ( Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher displays the Word Grid and requests that the students find the given words in the grid
(Annex-2). Each student who finds the given word is required to construct a sentence using “I like”, “love”,
“dislike”, or “hate” structures. This allows the teacher to assess whether the students can correctly use
these structures to express their likes and dislikes.
While Activity
Following the word grid activity, the teacher displays the My Weekly Game Schedule activity, in which
the students will follow a simple story with visual aids and construct sentences (Annex-3). The teacher
introduces the character named Sam and asks the students to work in groups of three to discuss
Sam’s game scedule. The teacher shares the link with the groups on a digital board and asks the
students to look at the images of the games and hobbies and construct sentences to tell the weekly
game schedule of Sam. The students may like or comment on the visual story. The students are asked
to construct sentences such as “Tom likes playing soccer with his friends on Tuesday.” The teacher
walks around the students during the discussions and provides prompt feedback throughout the
course of the activity.
Post-Activity
Following the discussion on the game schedule of Sam, the teacher proceeds to the assessment part
and shares the link to the Reflective Journal, where the students are asked to answer the questions to
check their self-development (Annex-4).
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates an activity named Horse Race using one of the gamified measurement and
evaluation web tools. Then, the teacher prepares another activity named Do You Like? using one of
the gamification web tools.

Let’s Play Together!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
3. Ders ( IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the Horse Race game, in which the students listen to audio clips such as
swimming, hide and seek, playing chess, etc., and match each with the corresponding image.The
teacher explains that the winner is the student who finds the answers the fastest. This interactive
game is intended to facilitate a warm start for the lesson.
While Activity
Following the horse race, the teacher initiates another activity titled Do You Like?, in which the students
look at the images and talk about hobbies and likes/dislikes and abilities in a simple way. The activity
includes images of games and hobbies such as skipping rope, riding a bike, roller skating, etc. The
teacher asks the students to work in pairs, one student poses the question “Do you like?”, the other one
chooses an option, and constructs sentences using “I like...”. The students then take turns to ask and
answer the questions. The teacher circulates amongst the students during the discussions, providing
immediate feedback throughout the activity.
Post-Activity
Following the activity, the teacher proceeds to the assessment part and shares the Reading Exercise:
Kevin’s Project on EBA. In this exercise, the students read the text and answer the questions.
Homework/Assignment
The students are assigned to work in groups of four to prepare a dialogue about their hobbies. They
are then asked to share their videos on EBA and comment on each other’s videos.
