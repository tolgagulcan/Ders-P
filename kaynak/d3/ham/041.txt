Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a list of relevant vocabulary related to tourism and cultural activities. The teacher
prepares a presentation titled Famous Landmarks of Türkiye including pictures of famous landmarks
in Türkiye.
1. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
To start the lesson, the teacher introduces a fun game of tourism trivia. The students are divided
into teams, and they answer questions about famous places and landmarks in Türkiye. This helps
them activate their prior knowledge about tourism and get excited about the unit to come. The
teacher initiates the Famous Landmarks of Türkiye presentation displaying pictures of famous places
in different regions of Türkiye. The teacher asks following questions to the students: “What do you
know about this landmark?”, “Why is this landmark famous?”, “Have you ever visited or heard of this
landmark?”, etc. and elicits answers.

While Activity
The teacher uses an artificial intelligence powered tool to prepare a presentation. The teacher enters
the following prompt to the AI tool ‘’Prepare a presentation about the most famous touristic places in
Türkiye. The presentation should include the historical and cultural importance of each place and its
interesting features for tourists. Also don’t forget to include visuals.” The teacher asks the students who
see different places in Türkiye to guess these places. The teacher then asks the students what kind of
places and touristic areas they would prefer to visit, considering the places in the presentation. The
students ask their classmates these questions to find out which places they would like to visit. They
can ask the following questions: “Do you like travelling?”, “What kind of holidays do you prefer?”, “What
places do you prefer to go? Why?”, “Where have you been in Türkiye?” They can use these phrases to
express their experiences: “Have you ever been to ...?”, “Yes, I have. /No, I have not.”, “I have been to Side
before.”, etc.
Post-Activity
Using a survey tool, the teacher asks the students to write down the places they would like to see the
most. The teacher analyses the data to identify popular destinations or common interests among the
students. The teacher leads a class discussion based on the collected data. The students share their
choices and explain why they want to visit certain places.
Before the Lesson
The teacher brings clues and riddles about famous landmarks from around the world that will challenge
the students and spark their curiosity. The teacher selects a suitable interactive presentation tool for
the students.
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces a landmark challenge to the students. The students are divided into teams.
The teacher presents a series of clues or riddles about famous landmarks as in the examples: “This
iconic tower is located in Paris.” (Answer: Eiffel Tower), “This ancient monument is located in Egypt.”
(Answer: Pyramids of Giza)”, etc. Each team is given a chance to guess the landmark. Correct answers
will be rewarded with points.
While Activity
The teacher asks the students to work in groups of three or four and create simple presentations
introducing their region's tourist attractions, local cuisine, and cultural highlights. Using an interactive
presentation tool, the students will share their findings and engage in collaborative work. The teacher
introduces a collaboration tool and encourages the students to share their presentations for everyone
to see. After they complete and share their presentations, the students from other groups will ask
questions about the presented region, such as, “What is the city famous for?”, “Where can we visit in
this city?”, “What can you taste in the city?” and “Which places do you suggest visiting? Why?”, etc.

3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher divides the class into groups. One student draws a visual of a word related to travel (e.g.
suitcase, map, beach) using a shape and drawing tool on the board, and the team members try to
guess the word within a time limit. The team with three correct words wins the game.
While Activity
The teacher assigns the students a writing task, such as writing a travel blog post about a fictional
trip or a review of a cultural event they attended. The teacher provides a list of vocabulary including
adventure, explore, culture, experience, “memorable and enjoyable for the students to incorporate into
their writing. The teacher encourages the students to use online resources such as dictionaries to find
synonyms and expand their vocabulary. The teacher then proceeds to walk around the classroom,
offering feedback on the students' writing and helping them to write a coherent piece of writing.
Post-Activity
Once the students have completed their initial drafts, the teacher requests that they exchange their
papers with a partner for peer review. This will help the students get feedback from their classmates
and improve their writing. To reinforce the students' vocabulary knowledge, the teacher initiates
Vocabulary Exercise 1: Tourism on EBA. The students complete the activity altogether.

Before the Lesson
The teacher downloads engaging videos showcasing different cultures, festivals, sustainable
tourism practices, and landmarks. The teacher informs the students about the evaluation process,
which involves the students’ active participation, pronunciation, grammar, engagement and ability
to describe places, express preferences, provide explanations, make comparisons, and share
experiences related to tourism and cultural activities (Annex-1).
4. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
To practise language skills and learn about different places, the teacher introduces a game to the
students. In the image description game, the teacher displays a series of images depicting different
tourist attractions or cultural landmarks. The students take turns describing the images using
vocabulary related to tourism and culture. The teacher evaluates their vocabulary knowledge and
expression on the topic.
While Activity
The teacher divides the students into small groups and provides each group with a list of tourism-
related vocabulary words. Then, the teacher assigns each group a different scenario (e.g. planning a
trip, booking accommodations, exploring a city) and has them create dialogues using the vocabulary.
The teacher encourages the students to use online resources like travel websites or apps to research
and incorporate authentic language into their dialogues. Each group prepares their dialogues by
brainstorming ideas, assigning roles, and practising their lines. On presenting their dialogues, the
students should use appropriate intonation, gestures, and facial expressions to convey their meaning
effectively. Each group performs their dialogues for the class. The teacher reinforces correct
vocabulary usage and provides additional examples. The teacher evaluates each group’s language
proficiency, vocabulary usage, and overall effectiveness and provides constructive feedback.
Post-Activity
The teacher observes the groups and fills in the form in the group work observation form to assess
the student's overall achievements in the unit (Annex-1). After completing the observation form, the
teacher provides individual feedback to each student, highlighting their strengths and areas for
improvement.
