Activities
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher prepares preposition cards, each labelled with a specific preposition
for the warm-up activity. The teacher creates a story containing multiple prepositions via an artificial
intelligence powered tool.
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher brings cards with prepositions and tells the students they will use their bodies to show
different prepositions. The teacher divides the class into small groups and gives each group a
preposition card. A group assigned “between” might have one student stand between two others,
linking arms.
For “under”, a student could crawl under a table, while the other stands nearby. Each group presents
their formation to the class without speaking. The teacher then leads a brief discussion, asking the
students to reflect on how the activity helped them understand the prepositions.
While Activity
The teacher writes a list of prepositions on the board. The teacher provides sample sentences to help
the students: “The book is on the teacher’s desk.”, “The pencil is in the pencil case.”, and “The bag is
under the chair.”

Write and Draw!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
The students make up two or three sentences using the classroom as inspiration. They think for a few
minutes, then share their sentences with the class. The teacher helps the students use prepositions
correctly and meaningfully. The teacher reads the text with prepositions and asks the students to
clap each time they hear a preposition. This activity fosters effective listening skills and expands the
students’ vocabulary.
Post-Activity
The teacher presents the text to the students on the interactive whiteboard, enabling them to view
the written form of all prepositions.
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher selects a song that features prepositions for the warm-up activity. The
teacher informs the students that they will be evaluated at the end of the activity and explains the
criteria for the evaluation. The teacher organises an evaluation form to assess group performance
at the end of the lesson (Annex-2).
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The students identify prepositions in a song by clapping or stomping. By actively participating in the
clapping or stomping, the students enhance their auditory discrimination and reinforce their grasp
of prepositional usage, integrating both kinaesthetic and auditory learning modalities.
While Activity
To integrate descriptive writing and visual interpretation, the class participates in an activity that
uses prepositions as they work in groups. The class is split into two groups: writing and drawing. The
writing group has to write a description of an imaginary room using prepositions. They might say,
“The bookcase is next to the bed” or “The wardrobe is between the table and the coffee table.”
The drawing group uses one of the visual creation and editing tools to turn the descriptions into
pictures. The drawing group reads the descriptions and draws a picture of the room based on
them. The drawing group must pay attention while listening for comprehension. Then the groups
switch roles. The drawing group writes a new paragraph while the writing group draws the room.
This rotation ensures that all the students practise both writing and drawing, which helps them to
understand prepositions.
Post-Activity
Following the completion of the writing and drawing tasks, the students share their final work on EBA
wall. The teacher and the students then review the work to correct any errors. The teacher subsequently
completes the evaluation form to assess the group’s performance (Annex-1).

Before the Lesson
Before the class, the teacher prepares two images that are almost the same, apart from a few key
details. These images can be used for a warm-up activity focused on prepositional phrases. The
teacher creates an AI-made image of a room containing a range of items suitable for detailed
description.
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher presents two similar images and highlights the differences between them. Some objects
in the first image are absent in the second. The students are asked to identify the missing objects
and their original locations.
While Activity
To improve speaking skills, the students are split into groups and shown an AI-generated image of a
room. Each group member describes one item from the image to the next student. The first student
says, “The lamp is on the bedside table,” and the second student says, “There is a plant next to the
lamp.” Each student describes one item at a time until the last student gives the full description. After
sharing the final descriptions, groups compare their original descriptions with the final verbal output.
This exercise helps the students understand what they hear and speak accurately.
Post-Activity
The students complete Grammar Exercise 2 from EBA for evaluation.
