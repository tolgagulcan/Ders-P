Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher uses a visual creation and editing tool to prepare the flags of various countries, turns them
into flashcards, prints them in colour, and brings them to the classroom. The teacher can also use
ready made Countries and Nationalities Flashcards prepared by using a visual creation and editing
tool (Annex-1). The teacher finds “Where are you from?” song and “Where are you from? Continents,
Countries & Nationalities” video for the lesson.
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher enters the classroom and greets the students in a language other than English. The
students wonder why they greet each other in this way. Some students may make a connection with
the topic. The teacher tells them they are going to play “Chinese Whispers” and asks the students to
line up. The teacher chooses a word from” another language that is used in greetings. For example,
the teacher chooses the word “Ciao” to say “Hello” in Italian. The teacher whispers the word to the
first student and asks them to continue the game. They whisper the word around until the last person
repeats it. The game is repeated, taking the time into account.
While Activity
The teacher indicates that the focus of today’s lesson will be on the examination of the names and flags
of various nationalities, with a particular emphasis on the facilitation of question-asking and question-
answering activities such as “Where are you from?” and “What nationality are you?” in English. To do
this, the teacher plays “Where are you from?” song. The teacher acts as a role model for the students
to repeat the lyrics.
After the song, the teacher distributes the Countries Nationalities Flashcards to the students (Annex-1).
When the name of the countries is mentioned in the song, the teacher asks whoever has the flag of
the correct country to raise the flag. While singing the song with the students once again, the students
who hold the flags in their hands follow the song better and raise the flag when it is their turn. While
collecting the flags after the song, the teacher asks the students again which country they belong to,
and they can repeat once again in this way.

Post-Activity
After the song, which allows the students to become a little more familiar with the words, the teacher
initiates “Where are you from? Continents, Countries & Nationalities” video in which people introduce
themselves. In the video, people say their names, countries and nationalities. After the sample video, the
teacher asks if there is anyone who can introduce themselves as in the video.
After the video and introductions, the teacher reviews the main points. They use these patterns: “Where
are you from? I am from Turkey.”, “What nationality are you? I am Turkish.” and “How many languages do
you speak? Two. Turkish and English.” They engage in a question and answer activity using different flags
to reinforce the topic.
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a matching activity named Countries-Nationalities with one of the web tools for
matching activities (Annex-2). The teacher prepares speaking cards as samples, outlining what the
students can mention in their video (Annex-3). The teacher can distribute them and let the students talk
about these cards or the students can research and collect their own information as in the scenario.
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the Countries-Nationalities matching activity for the students to categorise countries
and nationalities prepared with one of the web tools for matching activities before the lesson (Annex-2).
The students drag and drop the names of countries and nationalities into the correct box. In this activity,
they’ll practice telling the difference between countries and nationalities.
While Activity
After the matching activity, the teacher divides the students into two groups. One student from each
group takes turns naming the location of a country. The teacher gives a sticker to the student who finds
the location of the country first and asks the student to stick it on the country. The teacher calculates the
scores, announces the winner, and the class applauds. Through this activity, the names of the countries
and their locations on the map are practiced.
Post-Activity
After the game, the teacher asks the students to look at the map. The teacher chooses a sticker and asks
which country it represents. Then the teacher asks which of their friends identified the country. They use
these patterns: “Where is s/he from?”, “S/he is from...” and “I can speak English/German.” At the end of the
activity, the students are expected to be able to ask and answer about themselves and other people.
Homework/Assignment
The students are assigned the task of conducting research on a country for their homework. The students
should research a country’s flag, culture, common names and languages and dress in traditional clothes
to record a one-minute video about the country. The teacher will evaluate the students’ work and ask
them to evaluate their classmates’ videos.

Before the Lesson
The teacher uses a video editing tool to merge the students’ videos, creating a single material to be
watched together in the lesson. The teacher can name the video “Around the World”. The teacher evaluates
the students’ performance on the video with an assessment form (Annex-4). The teacher watches the
merged video and prepares a Sample Survey about this video with one of the web tools for e-surveys
(Annex-5). Since each class will have a separate video, questions should be prepared according to the
video.
3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher hangs the flag visuals on the board in order. The teacher tells the students that they will play
a memory game. Then the teacher encourages them to count the names of the countries in order. The
teacher takes the first flag, asks the studens to remember the flag in the 1 first place and encourages
them to count the rest. Then the teacher takes the 2 second flag. The students try to remember the first
2 rows. The game continues in this way. Towards the end, the students may have difficulty remembering
them all in order. The students who remember them all can be rewarded at the end of the warm-up
activity.
While Activity
The students watch the videos they prepared at home. The teacher tells the students that they will
complete an online survey about this video. The teacher asks the students to watch the video closely and
write down what they learn about their friends, their countries, and their languages.
Post-Activity
After watching the video, the teacher wants the students to complete the Sample Survey about video
(Annex-5). In this survey, questions and sentences related to the required outcomes are included. Thus,
the information discussed from the beginning is applied with the online survey. After applying the survey
in the lesson, the teacher shares the results with the students. The students review the correct answers
and discuss them with the teacher and their friends.
Homework/Assignment
The teacher assigns Grammar Exercise 5: Getting Info about Others to the students and asks them to
complete it within the specified time. The teacher checks the students who completed the activity on EBA
and the percentage of correct answers.
