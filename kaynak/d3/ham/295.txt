## 6. Sinif/Ingilizce/Occupations - Exploring Future Occupations.pdf

Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher identifies and utilises appropriate animation creation tools to facilitate the student
learning. The teacher informs the students of the criteria and process for evaluating homework and
prepares a student assessment form (Annex-1).
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher begins the lesson by reminding the students about professions, job descriptions, and
dates. “She is a teacher, she can teach English, but she can’t repair houses”, “She works on weekdays”,
“She was born on 12th June 1980”, “She was a student in 2004”, etc. The teacher wants the students to
discuss in pairs or small groups to list as many different job roles as they can within a set of time, such
as two minutes. Afterward, each group shares their lists with the class, and the teacher facilitates a
discussion on the variety of professions.
3
Exploring Future Occupations
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher facilitates a listening activity Listening Exercise: Occupations on EBA to enrich the
students’ vocabulary.
The teacher introduces an animation maker tool to demonstrate how to design characters, scenes,
and dialogues about professions to the students. Animation creation starts with choosing the
character and scene via the tool. After selecting the characters, the students select the scene,
and they vocalise dialogues by using the recording start button. The teacher initiates the tool on
the interactive whiteboard, which they decide on together with the students. The teacher divides
the students into groups of three or four. First, the students decide on the professions they want to
introduce, then they write the dialogues in their notebooks. The teacher checks and provide feedback
to the students throughout the process. The groups that are ready, start to create their content on
the interactive whiteboard. Each group saves its content after they create it.
Post-Activity
After all groups finish the activity, they watch animation videos together.
Homework/Assignment
The teacher asks the students to prepare the same activity individually as homework, with the same
rules. The teacher requests the students to upload the assignments to one of the collaboration tools.
Through the use of this tool, all the students and the teacher can see animation videos.
The teacher informs the students about the evaluation process and the criteria. The teacher evaluates
the student videos using the student assessment form, which includes 5 criteria: “Originality”,
“accuracy”, “use of information technologies”, “relevance to the subject” and “clarity”(Annex-1).
Before the Lesson
The teacher asks the students to bring costumes, materials, tools and equipment related to the
professions.
2. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher elucidates to the students that they will be engaging in a recreational dress-up relay
game to ascertain the correct designation of various professions. The teacher exemplifies the
activity by wearing one of the costumes and enacting a concise scenario pertinent to the profession
in question. For instance, the teacher can wear a lab coat and use a stethoscope to examine a
patient. After the activity, the teacher leads a discussion about the different professions represented
and their roles in the society. The students can share their observations and discuss the skills and
responsibilities associated with each profession.
4
Exploring Future Occupations
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher initiates the previous lesson task on the interactive whiteboard and assigns a number
to each video prepared by the students. The teacher uses a random selection method, such as
drawing numbers from a hat or using a random number generator, to choose which videos to
watch. This will ensure fairness and impartiality in the selection process. They watch the animation
videos together. After watching the animation videos, the students are divided into pairs or small
groups. The teacher asks the students to create short role-play skits where they act out scenarios
related to different professions introduced in the videos. For example, one group might portray a
doctor-patient interaction, while another group acts out a teacher-student dialogue. The students
can use props and costumes to make their skits more engaging.
The teacher leads the students to Grammar Exercise 5: Occupations and Grammar Exercise 6:
Occupations activities on EBA. The teacher along with the students completes the activity to reinforce
the course objectives.
Post-Activity
The teacher initiates a digital tool that displays the date on the interactive whiteboard. The dates
come randomly, the students say the dates and write the spelling in their notebooks.
The teacher initiates a reading activity Reading Exercise: Occupations on EBA and reads the text. Then,
the teacher chooses a few students and requests them to read the text aloud. The teacher selects
the students by calling out random numbers from the attendance list, ensuring a fair and engaging
process. The students are allowed time to look up unfamiliar words and answer questions about the
text. They then give their collective responses.
Homework/Assignment
As an assignment, the teacher shares Occupations 1 video on EBA with the students. The teacher
informs the students that they will evaluate the video with a web tool for multiple-choice questions
next lesson.
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a Exploring Future Occupations Quiz using one of the web tools for multiple-
choice questions (Annex-2). The teacher prepares a puzzle for Time Explorer Game by using one of
the gamified measurement and evaluation web tools (Annex-3).
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher launches a web tool for multiple-choice questions on the interactive whiteboard to
create Exploring Future Occupations Quiz based on the previous lesson’s homework video (Annex-2).
The students take turns and answer the questions.
5
Exploring Future Occupations
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher initiates Writing Exercise: My Dream Job on EBA. The teacher reads the sample writing
activity and answers the students' questions about the text. The teacher encourages the students to
write a similar text, skims the texts by walking between the desks, guides the students who need it, and
asks the students to help each other. At the end of the activity, the teacher allows all the students to
read their texts, starting with the voluntary students. In writing activities, the students may be reluctant
to read their texts. In this process, the teacher identifies the students’ areas of underperformance and
provides them with constructive feedback.
Post-Activity
The teacher initiates the Time Explorer Game including the unit outcomes; expressing dates, asking
personal questions and getting to know professions on the interactive whiteboard (Annex-3). During
the game, the students solve interactive puzzles related to unit outcomes, and the teacher monitors the
progress of the students and provides guidance when needed.
