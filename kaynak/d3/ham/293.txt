Activities
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher gathers all necessary materials, such as markers and sticky notes.
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher recounts a personal holiday experience to introduce new vocabulary as follows: “Last
month I was in the Black Sea region”, “There were lakes, mountains, forests and rivers”, “The landscape
was fascinating!”, “There were fruit trees and different flowers”, “The sea was refreshing, the sightseeing
was great and it was a wonderful holiday”, etc. The students are then asked to describe their own
holiday experiences as follows: “Where did you go on your last holiday?”, “Was it beautiful?”, “Were
there any great scenes or places?”, etc.
While Activity
Subsequent to an overview of the lesson being presented, the teacher initiates Listening Exercise: New
Friends. Throughout this activity, the teacher engages with the students to ensure comprehension of
the presented material.
After completing the listening exercise, the teacher remarks, “Great job, everyone! Let’s now move
on to expanding our vocabulary and see how well you can identify our new vocabulary words”. In
Vocabulary Exercise 1: Holidays, the students match photos with the appropriate words.
The teacher organises an activity related to the vocabulary of the unit. The teacher starts one of the
interactive presentation tools. The students in pairs describe and write the words of the unit by using
AI powered feature of the interactive presentation tool. Meanwhile, other students can draw similar
pictures in their notebooks.
Post-Activity
Upon completion of the task, the teacher presents the images, if feasible, and provides the students
with printed copies, which they then affix to the designated English-language learning area of the
classroom.
Homework/Assessment
The teacher provides the students with a link to Holidays video as homework, instructing them to
view it while taking notes on the subject matter in their notebooks. The students will discuss the topic
further in the subsequent lesson.

Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
Before the Lesson
The teacher gathers all necessary materials, such as sticky notes, markers, and notebooks. The
teacher prepares a Multiple-Choice Test on the web tools for multiple-choice questions, reviews the
AI-generated text about holidays, and converts it into a listening activity (Annex-1). The teacher also
tests the audio editing tool to ensure the students can easily use it for their homework assignment.
Additionally, the teacher informs the students to bring their materials, be prepared to share their
holiday experiences for the upcoming lesson. The students should also review the vocabulary from
the previous lesson and be ready for an engaging discussion on holiday destinations.
2. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher displays a world map on the interactive whiteboard and gives each student a sticky note
and a marker. The students will pin sticky notes with holiday destinations onto a map.This activity
encourages geographical awareness and stimulates discussions about different places around the
world.
While Activity
The teacher starts the activity, which is based on a web tool that presents multiple-choice questions
related to the video that was assigned as homework in the previous lesson (Annex-1). The students
record their answers, after which they verify them collectively. The teacher initiates Vocabulary
Exercise 2: Holidays on EBA, and the students match the words with their descriptions.
The students are introduced to past tense regular verbs through a listening activity for the first time.
To draw attention to regular verbs, the teacher uses an artificial intelligence powered tool to write
an original text about holidays according to the unit outcomes and context. The teacher turns the
text into a listening activity with an audio editing tool. After playing the audio two or three times, the
teacher displays the text on the interactive whiteboard. The students identify and note unfamiliar
words and structures. The teacher then prompts the artificial intelligence chatbot tool to generate a
new listening text focused on past tense regular verbs: “Can you write an authentic 150-word text on
the topic of holiday that I can turn into an audio text for my 6th grade students who will be learning
regular verbs in the past tense for the first time in English class?”
Homework/Assessment
As homework assignment, the teacher asks the students to record what they did during their last
holiday using an audio editing tool and upload it to the link provided by the teacher.
Before the Lesson
The teacher checks the students’ audio homework. The teacher sets assessment criteria for the
students, reviews grammar activities on EBA and designs a Verb Match Up Game using one of the
gamification web tools. The teacher announces an upcoming student assessment (Annex-2).

3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher switches on the audio recordings of the homework given in the previous lesson on the
interactive whiteboard and the students listen to them together. The teacher uses an assessment form
to evaluate the students’ recordings. The evaluation is based on “correct use of regular verbs”, “fluency”
and “pronunciation” (Annex-2).
While Activity
Following the student assessment, the teacher proceeds to the main grammar activities by initiating
Grammar Exercise 1: Holidays and Grammar Exercise 2: Holidays. The students are required to complete
these exercises all together. The teacher makes sure that the students pay attention to the use of regular
verbs in each question. Once the students have completed the exercises, they check their answers with
the teacher as a whole class. The teacher then prepares the follow-up activity.
Post-Activity
The teacher initiates the Verb Match Up Game to facilitate the acquisition of regular verbs through a
fun and engaging matching activity. The students match the verbs with the correct tense form on the
interactive whiteboard. This game serves as an effective learning tool for the students to have fun while
learning regular verbs using interactive whiteboard technology.
