Activities
Before the Lesson
The students independently research a chosen scientist, gathering information on birthdate,
birthplace, education, and awards. They create interactive presentations via one of the interactive
presentation tools to share findings with classmates. Presentations are shared on a classroom
management platform for early peer feedback. The teacher provides the students with the link to
the tool on EBA in advance of the scheduled lesson. The teacher creates The Life of Louis Braille as a
sample timeline using one of the poster and flyer creation tools.
1. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The students share all the information they have gathered on the classroom management tool
and after the discussion the teacher wants them to answer short answer questions. The students
describe a scientist they choose from their presentations and ask the question “Who am I ?” They try
to find the right scientist according to the description.

While Activity
The teacher uses a web tool for short answer questions to create a short assessment about biographical
clues (birthplace, achievements, etc.) about famous scientists. The teacher formulates the short
answer questions based on the subject matter researched by the students prior to the lesson. The
students share their answers online in a competitive format. The teacher facilitates brief discussions
about renowned scientists, prompting reflection through questions pertaining to the activity and the
figures researched, such as: “Please indicate whether you enjoy the activity. Did you search for any of
the scientists? If so, which ones?”
The teacher elicits language for describing people’s lives through class discussion. The students then
listen to Listening Exercises: Biographies from EBA, completing gap-filling exercises and answering
comprehension questions to enhance language learning.
Then the teacher initiates Vocabulary Exercise 1: Biographies from EBA. The students are required to
match the words with the correct pictures.
The students then complete the Vocabulary Exercise 2: Biographies on EBA, matching words to create
biography-related sentences.
Post -Activity
The students are introduced to a digital poster and flyer creation tool. The teacher presents The Life
of Louis Braille as an exemplary timeline. Subsequently, the students are tasked with constructing
timeline posters featuring scientists of their own selection. These posters will then be shared amongst
classmates through a collaborative online platform.
Before the Lesson
The teacher uses a digital story creation tool to create The Story of Marie Curie (Annex-1). This sample
digital story includes speech bubbles and questions. The teacher prepares a questionnaire with a
web tool for e-surveys before the lesson (Annex-2).
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher shares The Story of Marie Curie with the students (Annex-1). After watching the digital
story, the students answer questions like whether they like it, if it was easy to make, and why. They also
reflect on making speech bubbles like this.
While Activity
The students are required to create a four-page digital story with speech bubbles, which should
include questions about the person’s birthdate, birthplace, residence, and schools attended. The
following example illustrates this: “Where was s/he born?”, “S/he was born in ...”, and “Where did s/he
live?” The students share their digital stories on EBA, and they rate and comment on their classmates’
works. The students expand their digital stories to include details about marital status, awards, career,
and hobbies. They then share their completed stories with their classmates via one of the online
collaboration tools.

Before the Lesson
The teacher creates square bingo cards including past tense verbs and profession names related to
the biographies or historical dates. The teacher uses a gamification web tool to create a Timeline of
Historical Events game. The teacher prepares a success criteria form and informs the students that
they will evaluate their own performance (Annex-3).
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher hands out the bingo cards and tells them to play “Past Tense Bingo Game” with vocabulary
from the biographies and other past tense verbs. The students indicate the presence or absence of
a verb or sentence in the present tense by marking off squares as the teacher provides the relevant
information.
While Activity
The teacher uses gamification web tools to create a digital game where the students unscramble the
Timeline of Historical Events related to the biographies. The students are required to put the sentences
in the correct order. They complete the timeline online and get immediate feedback and this reinforces
chronological order.
The students discuss key vocabulary from the biographies, focusing on past tense verbs related
to achievements and life events. They practice constructing sentences using these verbs and
chronological order, guided by sentence starters provided by the teacher such as: “[Name] was born in
[Year]”, “[He/ She] achieved great things like...” and “[Name] died in [Year].”
Post-Activity
The teacher hands out the success criteria form at the end of the lesson and encourages the students
to reflect on whether they achieve the learning objectives or not (Annex-3). This form includes “can
do” statements aligned with the lesson objectives. The students assess their performance and identify
areas for improvement. The form can be digitally shared for efficient feedback. The teacher can create
and share success criteria form with the students using web tools for self-assessment.
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a short reading text about biographies. The teacher uses a gamification web
tool to create The Present and Past Activity. An exit ticket self-assessment form is prepared by the
teacher (Annex-4). The teacher provides the students with guidelines for their digital story assignment
(Annex-5).

The Story of a Scientist
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
4. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher hands out a short reading text about biographies and wants the students to draw pictures
about what they read.
While Activity
The teacher provides the present tense form of the words using gamification web tools and requests
the past tense form. These words comprise a selection of verb forms typically used in biographies. The
teacher requests the verbs and receives responses from the students. Following The Present and Past
Activity, the teacher divides the class into small groups and provides each group with a short biography
text of a different famous person. The teacher instructs the students to read the text carefully and to
identify the verbs in the simple past tense. The teacher asks the students to discuss the significance of
the events in the biography and how they contribute to the person’s life story. The teacher alternates
between the groups to provide assistance and clarify the situation if necessary.
Post-Activity
The teacher asks the students to share their biographies on the board or to present them for “a gallery
walk” in the classroom. The students can ask questions and learn about each other’s chosen figures.
The teacher shares the exit ticket form for self-assessment with the students (Annex-4). The students
are assigned to fill in the form about biographies until the next lesson. The students are required to
write down one question they have after the lesson and three facts about biographies.
Homework/Assignment
The teacher assigns the students to create a digital story that explores the biography of a significant
historical figure. The story should be engaging and informative. It should also demonstrate a clear
understanding of the chronological order and the historical significance of the person’s life and
achievements. The students should prepare a two-minute video by using one of the digital storytelling
platforms and share their homework on EBA (Annex-5).
