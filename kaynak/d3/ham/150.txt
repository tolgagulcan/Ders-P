Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a presentation titled Countries/Nationalities using an interactive presentation
tool (Annex-1). Additionally, the teacher generates a list of Countries/Nationalities/Languages using a
visual creation and editing tool, which is then shared on EBA. The teacher brings a ball to the class.
1. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The students form a circle, and the teacher stands in the middle. The teacher says “Hello!”, takes the
ball, calls out a name and throws the ball to a student. Each student introduces themselves and throws
the ball back to the teacher.
While Activity
The teacher initiates the Countries/Nationalities presentation on the interactive whiteboard (Annex-1).
The presentation consists of characters from different nations. The teacher introduces the characters
briefly in the presentation.The teacher reads the sentences giving personal information slowly and
asks the nationalities of each character. For example, after reading the sentences introducing Hans,
the teacher asks, “What nationality is Hans?” The teacher provides prompt feedback throughout the
course of activity.

Nice to Meet You!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
After introducing each character, the teacher writes “Hello! My name is Hans. I am from Germany, and
I am German. I can speak German.” on the board. The teacher underlines the words “Germany” and
“German” and asks the difference between them to the students. The teacher focuses on the difference
between country and nationality. The teacher presents the Countries/Nationalities/Languages list,
which has countries such as, “China, France and Italy, etc.”, on the interactive whiteboard and provides
sample sentences such as “I am from China. I am Chinese and I can speak Chinese and English”,
“Mike is from France. He is French. He can speak French and Spanish.” The students are then invited to
construct sentences in the same style.
Post-Activity
The teacher wants the students to work in pairs, to go over the list of Countries/Nationalities/Languages
on the interactive board and to choose a county for themselves to create an imaginary character. The
teacher assigns the students the task of researching the general characteristics (likes and dislikes) of
the nations they have chosen for their imaginary characters. The teacher asks the students to make
a good search before creating their imaginary characters by using a visual creation and editing tool
in the next lesson. After the lesson the teacher shares the Countries/Nationalities/Languages list on
EBA and wants the students to revise it.
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher finds a song titled “What’s your nationality?” to use as a warm-up
activity. The teacher prepares a peer evaluation form to be used at the end of the lesson (Annex-2).
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
With the help of the song, the teacher reminds countries, nationalities and their flags to the students.
The teacher asks the students to clap their hands when they hear a nationality in the song and, also
wants the students to watch out the colours of the flags while listening to the song.
While Activity
The students work in pairs to create an imaginary character using a visual creation and editing tool
ans add their voices by using an artificial intelligence powered tool to speak up their characters.
Groups must introduce their character, including country, nationality, languages and likes/dislikes.
After creating the image of the character and introducing the character, the teacher wants the
students to insert the flag of the character’s nation under the visual. While the students are working
on their characters, the teacher walks around the classroom to check their improvement. If the
students need help, the teacher can provide some clues. If the teacher is pleased with the students'
progress, the teacher says, “Good job!” to encourage them. The teacher provides an overview of the
next lesson’s activities, noting that these visuals will be printed, and utilised for the upcoming gaming
exercise. The teacher also informs the students that their works will be evaluated by their peers using
the peer evaluation form prepared by the teacher. When all the students complete their work, the
students introduce their characters to their classmates.

Post-Activity
The teacher distributes the peer evaluation form to the students and wants them to evaluate their

Homework/Assignment
As an assignment the teacher shares Grammar Exercise 3: Getting Info about Others on EBA. The teacher
asks the students to put the words in the correct order and if it is a question, to give an answer to it.
Cultural awareness
Before the Lesson
The teacher prints out the visuals of the imaginary characters created by the students in the previous
lesson. The teacher creates a few more Character Visuals of different nations using a visual creation
and editing tool and attach their flags under the pictures. Moreover, the teacher prepares a visual
titled Likes/Dislikes using a visual creation and editing tool to be used for the game.
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher provides a visual reminder of the flag colours of different nations by displaying images of
flags on the interactive whiteboard. Prior to initiating the activity, the teacher and the students conduct
a brief review of each flag.
While Activity
The teacher displays the printed Character Visuals such as, German boy, Chinese girl to the whole class
one by one slowly and wants the students to focus on the flag colours. Then the teacher initiates the
Likes/Dislikes visuals on the interactive whiteboard that include sentences about likes and dislikes, such
as, “I like sushi”, “I love animals” and “I hate drawing.” After presenting the whole visuals and showing the
sentences of likes and dislikes, the teacher explains the instructions clearly. The teacher announces
that one of the students will be responsible for guessing. One of the Character Visuals will be attached
to the back of the student, who will then turn back to their classmates. The student’s classmates help
the student to find the nationality of the character by giving clues, such as the colours of the flag under
the visual. When the student identifies the correct nationality and announces it, the teacher asks the
student to choose one like and one dislike from the Likes/Dislikes visual on the board. The student can
express extra likes and dislikes. This game continues until all the character visuals have been used.
When the students find the correct answer, the teacher gives the students a flag. The student with the
most flags becomes the winner of the game.
Post-Activity
The teacher hands out the character visuals to the class and wants the students to form a few more
sentences under the visuals about their countries, nationalities, likes and dislikes. The teacher reviews
the sentences and corrects any mistakes, and provides constructive feedback to help the students
improve.
