Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher is required to design various digital materials to enhance student engagement and
learning. First, the teacher designs an activity named Guess the Music using one of the gamification
web tools and downloads different types of music, such as “jazz, techno, and pop, etc.” to be used for the
activity. Then the teacher creates an animation titled Plans for the Weekend using one of the animation
creation tools (Annex-1). Lastly, for the assessment, the teacher developes Elizabeth’s Routines multiple-
choice questions using one of the web tools for multiple-choice questions (Annex-2).
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the Guess the Music activity, where the teacher plays the audio from the
interactive whiteboard and asks the students to guess the types of the music such as techno, classical,
jazz, etc. The student who gives the correct answer can dance to the music if they prefer.
While Activity
Following the activity, the teacher initiates the Plans for the Weekend animation, where the students are
required to identify the phrases of preference and expressions about regular activities of teenagers
(Annex-1). When the animation is over, the teacher asks, “What other activities do the teenagers engage
in?” The teacher wants the students to form sentences about their favourite free time activities,
favourite types of music and movies. Additionally, the students are required to form sentences about
the activities that they don’t like or prefer. The students are expected to use the structures such as, “I am
crazy about”, “I am fond of” or “I can’t stand” while talking about their activities and the teacher provides
prompt feedback throughout the course of the activity.
Post-Activity
Following the animation, the teacher employs multiple-choice questions derived from Elizabeth’s
Routines text to assess the students’ comprehension. The student who is the fastest and provides the
greatest number of correct answers is declared to be the winner (Annex-2).

Before the Lesson
The teacher designs a visual named Four Corners of Me using one of the visual creation and editing tools
to enhance student engagement. Additionally, for the assessment, the teacher is required to prepare a
group assessment form, where the students will evaluate their group members’ performance in creating
an animation (Annex-3).
2. Ders ( IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the Four Corners of Me activity, where the teacher displays a visual on the
interactive whiteboard, asks the students to form groups of four and to think of the four parts of
themselves such as “Something I am crazy about, something I can’t stand, etc.” The students brainstorm
for a while, share their answers with their classmates and comment on the answers.
While Activity
Following the activity, the teacher wants the students to continue with their group members to create
an animation using one of the animation creation tools, where the students are required to write short
and simple dialogues about regular activities of teenagers and make a weekend plan. Additionally,
the students are required to provide a simple description of their daily activities and to express what
they prefer, like and dislike while creating the animation. The teacher informs the students that their
performance will be evaluated by their group members at the end of the lesson. While the students are
working on their animations, the teacher walks around the classroom to check their improvement. If the
students need help, the teacher provides clues. If pleased with their progress, the teacher says, “Good
job!” to encourage them.
Post-Activity
When the students finish creating the animation, the teacher hands out the group assessment form
and asks the students to evaluate their group members (Annex-3). The teacher reviews the assessment
results with the students and provides relevant feedback.
Homework/Assignment
The teacher shares the Reading Exercise: Teen Life on EBA and assigns the students to read the passage
and to find the correct answers.

World of Teenagers
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
Before the Lesson
The teacher is required to design various digital materials to enhance student engagement and learning.
First, the teacher creates an activity titled How Do You Feel? using one of the gamification web tools.
Moreover, the teacher prepares a presentation titled Adjectives for Emotions using one of the interactive
presentation tools (Annex-4). Lastly, for the assessment, the teacher creates a matching activity named
Adjectives and Explanations using one of the web tools for matching activities (Annex-5).
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces the How Do You Feel? activity, where the teacher initiates the visual on the
interactive whiteboard, asks the students to choose a feeling from the visual that reflects their emotions
such as embarrassed or happy, then drag the marbles into the corresponding box and the teacher
then explains the reasons in a few sentences.
While Activity
After the activity, the teacher initiates the Adjectives for Emotions presentation, where the students
follow the provided adjectives and identify their meanings. (Annex-4). During the presentation of the
adjectives, the teacher writes additional examples on the board and invites the students to form
sentences to discuss their preferences using the provided adjectives.
Post-Activity
After the presentation, to assess the comprehension, the teacher implements the Adjectives and
Explanations matching activity, where the students are encouraged to match the adjectives with the
corresponding explanations (Annex-5).
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a sticky note using one of the tools for creating digital boards or post-its, where the
students are required to complete the assigned sticky note.
4. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces a miming and gesturing game designed to enhance student engagement and
recall of the adjectives, and free time activities of teenagers. The teacher asks for a volunteer student to
come to the board and use mimes, gestures, or body language to describe an adjective or a teenage
free-time activity, while the other students try to guess it. The student who guesses the correct answer
comes to the board to describe another adjective.

While Activity
After the miming and gesturing exercise, the teacher introduces a sticky note containing a sample daily
routine, free time activities, or hobbies. The teacher shares the sticky note with the students, explains
the task, and invites them to describe their routines or hobbies using both an emoji and an adjective to
express how each activity makes them feel. The sticky note prompts the students to share not only their
favorite activities but also their least favorite ones. The teacher supports the students as they plan their
routines and hobbies. Once completed, the students are encouraged to share their responses with
their classmates.
Post-Activity
Once the sticky notes have been completed and shared with their classmates, the students proceed
to assess their comprehension of the adjectives. They then attempt to identify the adjectives that
correspond to the emojis. They provide feedback on the emojis and share information with their
classmates.
