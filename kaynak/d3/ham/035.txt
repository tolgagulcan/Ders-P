Activities
Before the Lesson
The students are required to complete the Vocabulary Exercise 1: In the Kitchen activity on EBA.
The teacher prepares a Healthy Salad recipe using one of the digital story creation tools (Annex-1).
Additionally, an activity titled Kitchen Action Match-Up is created with one of the web tools for matching
exercises.
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher informs the students that they will be playing the “Whisper Challenge” game with added
music and dance elements for extra fun. The class is divided into two teams, and with music playing,
the teacher whispers sentences to the team leaders, such as “I love Dubai chocolate, but I don’t like
hamburgers,” or similar phrases. Each team leader then dances their way to the next teammate, who
receives the whispered message and repeats the process. The last student in each team announces
the message they received, revealing how much the sentence has changed along the way.
While Activity
Following the game, the teacher presents the Healthy Salad digital story, guiding students through
each step of the recipe (Annex-1). As the students watch the salad-making process step by step, the
teacher encourages them to ask questions and repeat key phrases to reinforce vocabulary, making the
learning process interactive and engaging.
Post-Activity
For the assessment, the teacher presents the Kitchen Action Match-Up activity, which includes words
such as slice, peel, pour, etc. In this task, the students are required to match each audio clip with the
correct picture.
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a Digital Chef Quiz using one of the gamified measurement and evaluation web
tools (Annex-2). The teacher also designs a digital story titled Dubai Chocolate Delight with the Digital
Chef using a digital story creation tool (Annex-3). Additionally, the teacher creates an activity titled
Order the Steps: Dubai Chocolate using one of the web tools for matching activities.

2. Ders ( IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher explains to the students that they will take a short interactive Digital Chef Quiz using one of
the gamified measurement and evaluation web tools to test their knowledge of kitchen-related English
vocabulary and cooking actions (Annex-2). This engaging, digital quiz introduces key vocabulary
and concepts in a fun, gamified way. Once all the questions are completed, the teacher reviews the
quiz together with the class, discussing the correct answers and clarifying any new vocabulary or
concepts.
While Activity
After the activity, the students read Dubai Chocolate Delight with the Digital Chef digital story, where
they explore the ingredients and the steps for making Dubai Chocolate (Annex-3). The teacher pauses
at specific points in the story to ask comprehension questions, such as “What ingredient do we add
next?” or “Why do we need to melt the chocolate?”. This helps ensure that the students understand the
content and encourages active engagement with the recipe process. After the animation activity, the
teacher asks the students to work in groups to create a food recipe animation. Each group is expected
to include the ingredients and steps in their animation, ensuring the recipe is clearly presented and
engaging. They should collaborate to explain the process using expressions such as first, second,
then, and finally to describe each step.
Post-Activity
The teacher transitions to the Order the Steps: Dubai Chocolate activity, which includes steps such as:
Firstly, melt dark chocolate in a bowl; secondly, add condensed milk and butter, stirring until smooth,
etc.
Pre-Activity
The teacher prepares an activity titled The Kitchen Spin utilising one of the gamification web tools.
The teacher also prepares another activity titled Guess & Type using a web tool for short answer
questions.
3. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The students are divided into three groups for the game “Kitchen Parasite”. Two groups, Group A (Tellers)
and Group B (Guessers), stand a few meters apart, facing each other. The third group, consisting
of eight members (Parasites), positions itself between the Tellers and Guessers with the mission of
blocking communication between the two groups. The teacher uses sentences related to cooking
steps from the kitchen unit, which the Tellers attempt to convey to the Guessers despite interference
from the Parasites. The teacher can use an hourglass to add a challenging element to the game.

While Activity
Following the game, the teacher asks the students to form sentences in the The Kitchen Spin activity,
which includes words such as dice, add, peel, etc. The teacher spins the wheel, and based on the two
words that appear, the students are required to make a sentence. This activity encourages the students
to practise speaking by using the given words to form sentences related to the kitchen and cooking.
Post-Activity
Following the speaking activity, the teacher transitions to the Guess & Type activity, which includes
words such as add, melt, bake, etc. The students are required to look at the pictures and type the
corresponding words. They see the first letter of each word as a hint, which helps them recall and write
the correct word. This activity focuses on writing skills, reinforcing vocabulary and spelling through a
fun, interactive exercise.
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a Kitchen Dart Challenge using one of the gamified measurement and evaluation
web tools (Annex-4). The teacher also prepares an Ingredients, Actions & Tools activity using a web
tool for matching exercises (Annex-5). Additionally, the teacher designs an Actions Quiz using a web
tool for multiple-choice questions (Annex-6). The teacher developes an evaluation form to provide a
structured and consistent way to assess the students’ interactive presentations (Annex-7).
4. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher explains to the students that they will take the Kitchen Dart Challenge (Annex-4) to test
their knowledge of kitchen tools. This engaging activity introduces key vocabulary in a fun, gamified
way. Once all the questions are completed, the teacher will review the answers, discussing the correct
responses and clarifying any new vocabulary.
While Activity
The teacher divides the students into teams of four and asks each team to write the ingredients and
recipe steps for a dish they know well, such as an omelette, pizza, or fruit salad. Each team is also required
to draw pictures on a poster to illustrate the ingredients or cooking steps and visually represent their
recipe. Once the writing is completed, each team’s leader presents their poster to the class, explaining
the ingredients and steps of their recipe.
Post-Activity
To assess the students, the teacher uses the Ingredients, Actions & Tools activity and asks the students
to place the ingredients, actions, and kitchen tools into the correct boxes (Annex-5). Following the
grouping activity, the students transition to the Actions Quiz, where they are encouraged to look at the
pictures and choose the correct verb (Annex-6).

Homework/Assignment
The teacher assigns the students to create an interactive presentation, including a recipe list and step
by step instructions for a dish of their choice. The recipe presentation evaluation form will be used
to assess the students’ creations (Annex-7). The students are encouraged to make the presentation
visually engaging by adding images, icons, or animations to illustrate each ingredient and cooking
step. This assignment allows the students to practise both digital skills and language, as they describe
the recipe process in an interactive format. After completing the task, the students will submit their
presentation to the teacher using one of the classroom management tools designated by the teacher.
