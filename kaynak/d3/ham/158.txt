Activities
Before the Lesson
The students are required to complete Vocabulary Exercise 1: The Animal Shelter on EBA. This exercise
involves matching various animal-related vocabulary items with their corresponding visual
representations, allowing the students to enhance their lexical knowledge and strengthen word-image
associations. The teacher prepares a Double Animals exercise using one of the gamification web
tools. The teacher designs an Animals activity using web tools for matching exercises, as well as a Zoo
Snapshots digital story using one of the digital story creation tools (Annex-1). The teacher creates a True
or False Challenge exercise for the digital story using one of the web tools for true-false questions.
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher integrates the Double Animals exercise as a warm-up exercise, requiring the students to
promptly identify the repeated animal. After the exercise, the teacher asks the students: “What is your
favourite animal?” to encourage engagement and spoken interaction.
While Activity
Following the gamification activity, the teacher introduces the Animals activity. In this task, the students
listen to audio clips and match each spoken word (e.g. cat, fox,) with the corresponding image. After
the matching activity, the teacher proceeds with the digital story Zoo Snapshots, which narrates the
observations captured by a wildlife photographer’s camera (Annex-1). Through the story, the students
listen to the names of animals, their offspring, and the actions in which they are engaged. To enhance
speaking skills, the teacher pauses the video at key moments to ask the students questions about
previous scenes, encouraging them to recall and describe what they have seen. At the end of the story,
the students engage in a pair-work activity where they take turns asking and answering questions
about the events and characters in the video, reinforcing their comprehension and speaking practice.
Post-Activity
For the assessment, the teacher transitions to the True or False Challenge exercise. The teacher informs
the students with the following instructions: “Items will fly by at a fast speed. See how many you can get
right before time runs out.” When the students identify a statement as false, the teacher challenges the
students to correct it by asking follow-up questions such as, “If the duck is not jumping, what is it doing?”,
fostering deeper thinking and speaking practice.

Before the Lesson
The teacher designs a game titled What’s Behind? and a Listen and Match activity using one of the
gamified measurement and evaluation web tools. The teacher prepares a Mother and Baby Animals
activity using web tools for matching exercises. Additionally, the teacher creates an Animals and Their
Babies digital story using one of the digital story creation tools (Annex-2).
2. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
As a warm-up activity, the teacher informs the students that they will play the game titled What’s
Behind?. The students drag the cursor over the screen to reveal the hidden image. This activity helps
engage the students and activates prior knowledge.
While Activity
After the gamification activity, the teacher proceeds with the Mother and Baby Animals activity. In this
activity, the students match mother animals with their corresponding baby animals. For example, cat–
kitten, dog-puppy, etc. After completing the matching activity, the teacher continues with the story
Animals and Their Babies, in which the students introduce the offspring of various animals (Annex-2).
To foster collaboration, communication, and interaction amongst the students while emphasising
speaking skills, the teacher organises a group activity after watching the video. The students discuss
and exchange ideas about the offspring of other animals in small groups. To enhance engagement,
the teacher can integrate the Fishbowl technique, where a central chair is placed in a circle, and the
students take turns stepping in to share their thoughts before rejoining the circle.
Post-Activity
For the assessment, the teacher continues with the Listen and Match exercise. The students are required
to listen to the sentences and match them with the corresponding images. The activity continues until
all answers are matched.
Before the Lesson
The teacher designs a Guess the Animal game using one of the gamification web tools. The teacher
prepares a People at the Moment exercise using one of the web tools for matching activities. The
teacher creates an Animals Quiz using one of the web tools for multiple-choice questions (Annex-3).
3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher informs the students that they will engage in the Guess the Animal game as a warm-
up activity. The students are tasked with dragging and placing letters into the correct positions to
unscramble the word or phrase.

While Activity
The teacher transitions to the People at the Moment exercise, where the students are required to drag
each description under the correct picture and click on them to zoom in. After completing the exercise,
the teacher asks the students to work in pairs to create a poster about a fictional character and describe
the work they are doing at that moment, similar to the activity. Each poster should include at least three
sentences. Once the groups have finished, they present their posters, and their classmates provide
feedback.
Post-Activity
As part of the assessment, the teacher conducts the Animals Quiz where the students have to choose
the correct answer (Annex-3).
Homework/Assignment
The teacher assigns the students to prepare posters about animals. Each student will introduce at least
two animals on their poster, including information about their names, what they eat, and any interesting
facts. The students can use digital tools or create their posters by hand with drawings and colours. This
assignment encourages creativity and personal expression while helping the students develop their
research and writing skills. It also reinforces vocabulary and knowledge about animals in an engaging
way.
