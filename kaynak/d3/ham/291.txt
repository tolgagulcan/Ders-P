Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a Holiday Types presentation by using one of the interactive presentation tools.
The teacher prepares a story summary form (Annex-1).
1. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the Holiday Types presentation and provides an explanation. The teacher leads a
holiday vocabulary game involving scrambled words and clues. The students compete to unscramble
holiday-related words and phrases working in teams by using clues provided by the teacher. The first
team guessing the correct one earns one point.

Draw Your Holiday
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher informs the students that in this lesson, they will collaborate in groups to create stories
using past tense patterns. These stories will then be transformed into audio clips using artificial
intelligence powered tools. To inspire the students, the teacher presents images of different vacation
types. Each group then selects a vacation type and begins writing a story about it. The teacher
provides a story summary form to guide the students in their writing process (Annex-1). The students
are encouraged to use dictionaries, seek help from the teacher or their groupmates, and apply their
knowledge of past tense patterns.
Post-Activity
Each group writes their own story, following the provided story structure outline. After completing
their stories, the teacher explains that they will be converting these stories into audio clips. If the
school lacks the necessary equipment, one group member can read their story aloud for the class.
Before the Lesson
The teacher reminds the students that after listening the audio clip they will illustrate the stories by
one of the shape and drawing tools in this lesson. The teacher prepares a group evaluation form
(Annex-2).
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher introduces key vocabulary relating to the vacation theme, such as destination, journey,
beach, mountains, activities, souvenirs and river. The teacher then requests that the students discuss
their preferred vacation activities, thus enabling them to apply the new vocabulary in context. This
preliminary exercise serves to engage the students and prepare them for the main lesson.
While Activity
After listening to their own stories and those of their classmates, the students will complete the
story summary form distributed in the previous lesson, ensuring they include specific details in their
drawings. They will also have the opportunity to ask questions to other groups.
The students will listen to other groups’ audio stories. While listening, each group will take detailed
notes. The teacher will then guide the students in creating visual representations of the stories by
using one of the visual creation tools based on their notes. The students have the option to utilise the
shape and drawing tool to generate their illustrations. Alternatively, they may choose to draw their
images directly on their notebooks.
Subsequently, the teacher assigns a timeframe for the students to create their illustrations. Upon
completion, each group presents their illustrations to the class, and all the students review both the
stories and illustrations. Finally, the teacher completes a group evaluation form prepared with one of
the web tools for group assessment (Annex-2).

Post-Activity
After analysing the evaluation forms, the teacher identifies the group with the highest score. To ensure
easy access to the stories and illustrations, the teacher creates a QR code linking to the content. This
QR code is then shared on EBA, making it accessible to all the students in the class. The students can
scan the code to access the content at any time.
Before the Lesson
The teacher finds a sample writing text on EBA and prepares for the game they will play in the class.
The teacher designs word cards and brings them to the class.
3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The students are divided into pairs or small teams. The activity begins with a holiday-related word,
such as “beach”. Each pair then takes turns saying words associated with the given topic, attempting
to complete as many words as possible within a given time frame. These words could include sand,
waves, sunscreen, shells, and surfing. The objective of this exercise is to enhance vocabulary and
stimulate creative thinking related to holiday concepts.
While Activity
The teacher shares Writing Exercise: Colarado as a sample text with the students to improve writing
skills. The students read and analyse the sample text about a holiday experience. They brainstorm
their own memories and plan their narratives using the techniques from the sample. After writing
independently, the students engage in peer review and share their work with the class. This process
enhances their writing abilities, allows them to share their personal experiences, and facilitates
learning from one another.
Post-Activity
After the writing activity, in order to reinforce holiday vocabulary the students play a game of charades.
Teams act out words or draw pictures related to the holiday. The first team to guess correctly earns a
point. This game encourages teamwork, creativity, and vocabulary development.
Homework/Assignment
The students are required to explore a holiday from a different culture through research. They can
choose to share their findings in a report, presentation, or creative work.
