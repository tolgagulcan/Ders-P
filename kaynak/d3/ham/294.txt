Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher assigns the students to think of a routine day, their repeated actions and their free time
activities. The teacher wants the students to focus on verbs to describe their actions. Thanks to this
assignment, the students get the preliminary information about the basic routines and free time
activities, and this makes it easy to follow the lesson. Additionally, the teacher prepares Routines
Flashcards by using visual creation and editing tools (Annex-1). Lastly, the teacher prepares a Find
Routines activity by using a web tool for matching activities and uses the activity for evaluation
(Annex-2).

I Like My Routines!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the lesson with a game named “Names and Adjectives”. The teacher wants the
students to think of an adjective to describe themselves. The adjective must start with the same letter
as their name. For instance, “I am Fatma, and I am funny.” After all the students tell their names and
adjectives, the teacher says “I am funny, I like my routines and also free time activities are very important
for me.” to draw attention to the new topic. “Without the free time activities and the hobbies, the life will
be more boring.” says the teacher and asks the students “What are your routines and hobbies? What do
you do in your free time?”.
While Activity
The teacher initiates the Routines Flashcards on the interactive whiteboard (Annex-1). The teacher
focuses on each flashcard and asks the students about their routines. The teacher shares the Routines
Flashcards on EBA and wants the students to go over the flashcards for a few times (Annex-1). After
completing the flashcards, the teacher asks the students to keep the routines in their mind for the game
they will play. The teacher explains the game instructions clearly. The teacher says that one of the
students will come to the board and the student will choose a routine to describe to the other classmates.
While describing, the student cannot speak. The students can just use mimes or body language. Who
knows the correct routine comes to the board and tries to describe another routine. The student who
gives the most correct answer becomes the winner and the teacher gives a printed routine flashcard
as a prize. The students are instructed to continue playing game until the routines have been sufficiently
consolidated.
Post-Activity
Once the required vocabulary has been sufficiently revised, the teacher initiates the Find Routines
activity on interactive whiteboard and wants the students to come to the board, drag and drop the
correct routine to match it with the correct picture (Annex-2). The teacher provides feedback to the
students when they achieve the correct matches.
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher prepares a presentation of Talking about Routines with the help of
interactive presentation tools (Annex-3). Additionally, the teacher creates a sticky note by using a tool
for creating digital boards or post-its. Lastly the teacher finds a song named “Hours of the day” about
the daily routines to use as a warm-up activity.
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
Thanks to the “Hours of the day” song, the students not only remember the routines in a day but also have
fun and feel ready for the class. While listening to the song, the teacher wants the students to imitate the
mentioned routine by using mimes or body language and in this way the teacher aims to see whether
the students can recognise phrases, words and expressions related to repeated actions or not.

I Like My Routines!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher initiates the presentation of Talking about Routines with the help of an interactive
presentation tool (Annex-3). The teacher focuses on the sample sentences and emphasises the
rules of forming a correct sentence. After all positive, negative and question forms are exemplified,
the teacher forms extra different types of sentences by using different subjects. The teacher draws
attention to the different usage between the singular and plural subjects. To make the difference
clear, the teacher writes simple sample sentences on the board by using the same verbs. For
instance, “I get up at 7 a.m.” and “Ann gets up at 7 a.m.” and emphasises the suffix “-s, -es, -ies”. To
deepen understanding, the teacher asks the students to form sentences of their routines by using
simple present tense. The teacher shares the Talking about Routines presentation on EBA and asks the
students to repeat the subject as much as possible (Annex-3).
Post-Activity
The teacher creates a sticky note by using a tool for creating digital boards or post-its. The teacher
displays the sample sticky note including a casual morning routine and shares the morning routine
sticky note with the students. The sticky note starts like “I get up at 7 in the morning. I wash my
hands and face, brush my teeth. After I have my breakfast...” The teacher asks the students to work
individually on the sticky note and to continue to write their routine day at least with five sentences.
The teacher helps the students to write their routines. When the students have finished, they share
their work with their classmates, who can like or comment on it.
Homework/Assignment
As an assignment the teacher shares Grammar Exercise 1: Life on EBA. The teacher asks the students
to match the pictures with the correct sentences.
Before the Lesson
Prior to the lesson, the teacher uses artificial intelligence powered tools to write and record the daily
routine of a famous person. The teacher may use prompts such as “Write the basic routine day of Aziz
Sancar with ten sentences by using simple present tense.” or “Write an ordinary day of Messi with ten
sentences.” The teacher prepares a peer assessment form to be used as an evaluation at the end of
the lesson (Annex-4).
3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher initiates the lesson with a video titled “Morning Routines of Highly Successful People”. After
watching the video, the teacher asks, “What is the importance of our routine?” and tries to emphasise
the significance of a planned routine day.

I Like My Routines!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
While Activity
The teacher shows the daily routine of a famous person created by artificial intelligence powered
tools. With assistance from an artificial intelligence powered tool, the teacher guides each student
through an audio text, which is played through headphones. The teacher goes over each sentence
to remind the students the usage of simple present tense and the verbs to state routines. Following
a thorough reading and listening of the script, the teacher provides instruction to the students to
work in pairs to create an illustration of the script. The teacher explains to the students what to do in
detail and reminds that their works will be evaluated by their pairs. The teacher asks students to read
the script again and dream on the routines mentioned. After the students dream of the character’s
daily routine, use a digital story creation tool to illustrate their character’s routine. While the students
are working on their stories, the teacher walks around the classroom, observes the works, provides
feedback and helps the students if they need.
Post-Activity
The teacher distributes the peer assessment form to the students and wants them to evaluate their
workmate (Annex-4). The teacher discusses the results of the assessment with the students and
provides the necessary feedback.
