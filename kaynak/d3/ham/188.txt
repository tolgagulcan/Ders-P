Activities
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a matching activity titled Special Days using one of the web tools for matching
activities. Then, the teacher creates a story named Fancy Dress Party using one of the digital story
creation tools (Annex-1). Finally, for the assessment, the teacher prepares multiple-choice questions
titled Quiz Time using one of the web tools for multiple-choice questions (Annex-2).
1. Ders (Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher begins the lesson by asking the students, “Do you like spending time with your friends and
your family?” and “How often do you meet your friends and for what purpose?” Then, the students are
invited to contemplate the significance of spending time with their friends and relatives. Subsequent to
the discourse, the teacher initiates the Special Days matching activity on the interactive whiteboard.
In this activity, the students are required to come to the board in pairs, view visuals such as a birthday
party or a slumber party and match them with the corresponding special day. The objective of the
activity is to draw attention to the importance of special days in our lives, and to emphasise the role
of friends and family.
While Activity
Following the matching activity, the teacher initiates the Fancy Dress Party story, in which the students
identify the phrases related to organising a party and the structures used for making suggestions
(Annex-1). The students are then invited to work in groups of three to discuss the following questions:
“Why do you think organising a party is challenging? Can you explain with examples? “Can you design
a themed party plan and explain each step and the reason behind it?”, “What might happen if we
skip planning before an event? Imagine a scenario and explain.” These questions are designed to
encourage critical thinking, active listening, and structured speaking in an engaging way. Throughout
the activity, the teacher provides ongoing feedback to support student participation and progress.
Post-Activity
Following the discussion, the teacher implements the Quiz Time multiple-choice questions to check
the comprehension, in which the students are required to work in pairs to answer the questions related
to the Fancy Dress Party story (Annex-2).

Come and Join the Party!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a visual titled Sequence Chart using one of the visual creation and editing tools,
and then prints it out and cuts it into sentences (Annex-3). Next, the teacher creates a template link titled
Fancy Dress Party Template using one of the digital story creation tools. Finally, the teacher designs a
digital board named Contribute to the Organisation using one of the tools for creating digital boards
or sticky notes.
2. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher employs a sentence strip sequencing activity to facilitate the students’ comprehension
of the sequence of actions involved in organising a party. The teacher distributes jumbled sentences,
and the students work in groups of four to arrange the strips in a sequence from planning the guest
list to cleaning up after the event. The first group to complete the sequencing task is asked to read the
sequence verbally. Meanwhile, the teacher displays the Sequence Chart on the interactive whiteboard
to verify its accuracy (Annex-3). Finally, the students are required to formulate sentences describing
how to organise a party in the correct order, using time connectors such as “first, then, and finally”.
While Activity
Following the sentence strip sequencing activity, the teacher shares the template link for the Fancy
Dress Party Template activity with the students. The students are then tasked with helping Cyber Ninja
in organising the rest of the party and completing the story. The teacher divides the class into five
distinct groups. The first group will write the invitation cards; the second group will arrange the cake
and beverages; the third group will sort out the decorations; the fourth group will take care of the
music; and the fifth group will organise everything and check that everything is ready. At the end
of the lesson, the whole class will work together to create a product. The students will contribute to
the collective endeavour within the larger group, while concurrently engaging in smaller groups. The
teacher provides ongoing feedback to facilitate the students’ engagement and progress throughout
the activity.
Post-Activity
The teacher shares the link of the digital board Contribute to the Organisation with the students and
asks them to share their group work on the digital board. The students need to provide feedback
and give suggestions to their friends’ stories using the structures such as, “How about…?” and “What
about…?”

Come and Join the Party!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
Before the Lesson
The teacher prepares a fill-in-the-blank activity titled Choose the Quantifier, using one of the web tools
for fill-in-the-blank questions (Annex-4). The teacher then creates a video called Final Checks using
one of the video editing tools and shares it on the EBA (Annex-5). In preparation for the subsequent
lesson, the students are expected to feel ready for a role-play activity in a party by watching the
provided video. They are also required to bring any props or materials necessary for their role play.
3. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher uses the Choose the Quantifier activity as a warm-up. In this exercise, the students work
in pairs, filling in the blanks with the correct quantifiers and revising what they have learnt (Annex-4).
For further practice, the students should form an additional sentence with the quantifier.
While Activity
Following the fill-in-the-blanks activity, the teacher initiates the Final Checks video, in which the students
recognise the quantifiers and phrases related to suggestions (Annex-5). Upon completion of the video,
the teacher requests that the students convene in groups of six to engage in a role-play activity. It is
the responsibility of Cyber Ninja and Mr. Do-It-All to undertake the final checks, greet their guests, and
engage in recreational activities with their guests. Groups are expected to devise a surprise ending
for the party, record their role-play and share it on EBA. In the process of planning the role play, the
students are required to use quantifiers, make suggestions, and accept or refuse them. The teacher
circulates amongst the groups, offering continuous feedback to encourage and progress among the
students throughout the activity.
Post-Activity
The students observe the role plays performed by their peers on EBA and provide feedback by liking or
commenting on these performances. The students are invited to vote for the ending they consider to
be the most creative, and for the performance they deem to be the best.
Before the Lesson
The teacher creates a word cloud titled Words for Celebration using one of the word cloud creation
tools. The teacher then designs a Checklist using one of the visual creation and editing tools and
shares it on EBA (Annex-6).

Come and Join the Party!
Technology-Enhanced Learning Scenarios
4. Ders (IT Class)
Pre-Activity
The teacher then presents the Words for Celebration word cloud, which contains vocabulary related to
celebrations and special days. Following this, the students are required to form groups of six students
and create an additional word cloud. This should consist of words related to the celebrations and
types of special days.
While Activity
The students will continue to collaborate within their existing groups and are assigned the task of
planning a surprise birthday party for a friend. They are expected to prepare a presentation outlining all
the necessary preparations and steps for the event, using one of the interactive presentation tools. The
students collaborate by assigning specific responsibilities to each member, and the presentations will
then be shared on EBA. As part of the task, the students are specifically required to write an invitation
card, include an offer, respond by accepting or refusing the offer, and use quantifiers to express
amounts. The students are informed that they will present their work to their classmates at the end
of the lesson. Innovative ideas will be voted on and awarded. The students employ the Checklist upon
completion of their presentations to check the steps. The teacher circulates amongst the students,
offering guidance and feedback as required.
Post-Activity
The students are invited to deliver their presentations, with the objective of persuading their peers of
the distinctiveness and originality of their ideas.
Homework/Assignment
The students are assigned to complete the Reading Exercise: Jue on EBA. The students are required to
read the passage and answer the questions.
